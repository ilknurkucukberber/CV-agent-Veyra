# Design System: CV Agent

## Creative North Star

**The Intelligence Core**

CV Agent feels like a cinematic AI product built for ambitious professionals.
An iconic glass intelligence core visualizes the system working behind every
decision. Carbon creates depth, mineral white provides editorial hierarchy,
and violet/indigo light signals intelligence, momentum, and decisive action.

## Core Rules

- One dominant statement per screen.
- Violet and indigo are luminous signals, never flat decoration.
- Depth comes from tonal separation, hairlines, and light—not soft floating cards.
- Motion communicates analysis, progress, and system state.
- Interfaces remain fully legible when motion is reduced.

## Motion Language

- **Mask reveal:** Major headings uncover with a controlled cinematic wipe.
- **Signal sweep:** A narrow cobalt light traverses selected surfaces.
- **Trajectory:** Dashed paths animate only where they explain career progress.
- **Status pulse:** Small indicators breathe slowly for live system state.
- **Route entrance:** Content settles once with short opacity and translation.

No bounce, elastic easing, decorative floating objects, or constant high-frequency
movement. Animation should feel engineered rather than playful.

## Layout

The public entry uses a disciplined split composition with editorial scale on the
left and a compact credential surface on the right. Authenticated screens use a
narrow graphite navigation rail, strong horizontal rhythm, and asymmetric
information density. Mobile collapses to a single hierarchy without losing the
statement, path, or primary action.

## Components

### Primary action

Violet-to-indigo gradient, mineral-white text, 12px radius, high-contrast focus
ring, dimensional glow, and a restrained light sweep on hover.

### Operational surface

Graphite background, one-pixel mineral-white hairline at low opacity, 6px radius,
and no decorative shadow. Selected surfaces may receive a cobalt top rule.

### Inputs

Carbon field, graphite border, mineral-white value, persistent label. Focus uses
a cobalt border and compact cobalt halo.

### Navigation

Carbon rail with muted labels. Active routes use mineral-white type, a cobalt
indicator, and a slightly raised graphite field. Icons remain stable and precise.

## Do / Don't

**Do:** use editorial scale, real data hierarchy, purposeful signal motion,
restrained contrast, and technical microcopy.

**Don't:** use rainbow palettes, playful blobs, pill-heavy interfaces, cartoon
motion, soft lifestyle-blog styling, or invented performance claims.
